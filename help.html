Help & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you need? Contact the front desk.

Booking a Court

How do I book a court?
Click "Book now" on the homepage, pick a date and time slot, enter your name and phone, choose payment method, and confirm.
Do I need an account?
No. Bookings are guest-based. You'll get a reservation code — save it to manage your booking later.
How far in advance can I book?
Up to 30 days ahead. Check the schedule page for available dates.

Payment

What payment methods are accepted?
Cash at the court, or online via GCash or Maya. Choose when booking.
When do I pay if I choose cash?
Pay at the front desk when you arrive for your session. Show your reservation code.
What if my online payment doesn't go through?
Your booking stays in "Awaiting Payment" status. You can retry from your reservation page or pay cash at the court.

Your Reservation

How do I find my reservation?
Click "My reservation" in the top nav. If you've booked before on this device, it'll open automatically. Otherwise, enter your reservation code.
Can I change or cancel my booking?
Yes — from your reservation page. Changes depend on availability. Cancellations are free up to 2 hours before your slot.
What do the statuses mean?
Awaiting Cash Payment — pay at the court. Awaiting Payment — online payment pending. Pending Confirmation — staff verifying payment. Confirmed — all set. Cancelled — booking cancelled.

Chat with Staff

How do I contact the front desk?
From your reservation page, use the chat panel to send messages or upload payment proof (screenshots). Staff typically respond within minutes during open hours.
Can I send photos?
Yes — attach screenshots of your GCash/Maya payment confirmation. This helps staff verify faster.

Still Need Help?

Visit the front desk during open hours (6 AM – 10 PM daily) or call the facility directly. We're happy to assist.
